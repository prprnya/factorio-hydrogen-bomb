# Hydrogen Bomb

Adds a hydrogen bomb to Space Age that unlocks in the late game, with a much larger explosion radius and much greater damage than the atomic bomb. Quality is recommended; otherwise, vanilla weapons lack the range needed to fire it safely.

## Features

- A new hydrogen bomb for the rocket launcher, Spidertron, and rocket turret.
- A much larger explosion radius and much greater damage than the atomic bomb.
- Preserves the vanilla nuclear explosion style and surface-specific effects.
- A new in-game achievement, *A thousand suns*, for firing a hydrogen bomb.

## Progression

- **Technology prerequisites:** Atomic bomb and Fusion reactor.
- **Research cost:** 2,000 × 60 s, using automation, logistic, chemical, military, utility, space, metallurgic, electromagnetic, agricultural, and cryogenic science packs.
- **Recipe:** 50 s, using 1 Atomic bomb, 10 Fusion power cells, and 10 Quantum processors.

## Safety

**With Quality enabled:** Only legendary-quality versions of the vanilla rocket launcher, Spidertron, and rocket turret can fire the hydrogen bomb from beyond its explosion radius at maximum range.

**With Quality disabled:** None of these vanilla weapons can fire the hydrogen bomb from beyond its explosion radius.

**Even at legendary quality:** Firing at a closer target can place the firing position inside the explosion radius.

## Compatibility

Other mods may add compatible weapons or alter the ranges of existing ones, so their safe firing conditions may differ from the vanilla behavior described above.

## Dependencies

- **Required:**
    - Factorio 2.1
    - Space Age
- **Recommended:**
    - Quality — optional. Only at legendary quality, the vanilla weapons can fire the hydrogen bomb from beyond its explosion radius at maximum range.
