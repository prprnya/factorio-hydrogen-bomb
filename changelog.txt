Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-07
  Features:
    - Late-game hydrogen bomb rocket ammunition for Factorio: Space Age, with twice the vanilla atomic bomb's damage and blast scale.
    - Vanilla nuclear explosion visuals and planetary surface effects.
    - Recipe: 1 atomic bomb, 10 fusion power cells, and 10 quantum processors, with a crafting time of 50 seconds.
    - Technology requiring atomic bomb, fusion reactor, and legendary quality, with 2000 research units at 60 seconds each.
    - Cyan fusion-themed ammunition icon and a technology icon combining the atomic bomb and fusion power cell.
    - English and Simplified Chinese localization.
